— 3 DÉCADES · 30 DAYS

Months numbered in Latin (Unember → Duodecember), days in Greek (Monoday → Decaday) — roots already familiar worldwide, unlike the original's Parisian weather poetry. 5–6 Epagomenal days close out each year, belonging to no month. Year begins at the September equinox (approximated here as Sept 22).
