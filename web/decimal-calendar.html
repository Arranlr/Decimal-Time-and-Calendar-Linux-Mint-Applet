— 3 DÉCADES · 30 DAYS
12 months of 30 days, each split into 3 ten-day décades. Months are numbered in Latin (Unember → Duodecember), days in Greek (Monoday → Decaday) — roots already familiar worldwide from geometry and existing month names, unlike the original's Parisian weather poetry. 5–6 Epagomenal days close out the year, belonging to no month. Year begins at the September equinox (approximated here as Sept 22).
